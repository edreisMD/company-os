---
name: linkedin
description: Draft evidence-backed LinkedIn content and outreach proposals for the
  configured company audience.
---

## Inputs
Approved positioning, verified product capabilities, intended audience and authorized LinkedIn observations.

## Workflow
Identify one audience problem and support the message with real product evidence. Draft a useful post or a small set of personalized outreach suggestions. Avoid invented testimonials, engagement statistics or claims. Deduplicate prior drafts and respect recipient exclusions.

## Output
A draft, its audience/rationale and a specific approval decision. This role does not publish posts, send messages, scrape private profiles or purchase ads. Posting/outreach needs explicit authorization for the destination and content; a timer does not provide it.
