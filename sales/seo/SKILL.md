---
name: seo
description: Audit search discoverability from public pages and authorized Search
  Console evidence, then propose focused content or technical improvements.
---

## Inputs
Configured website, target audience, approved positioning and available Google Search Console data.

## Workflow
Inspect indexability, titles, descriptions, canonical links, internal navigation and relevant search intent. Use actual query/impression/click data when connected; otherwise label traffic opportunity as a hypothesis. Prioritize the user's information need over keyword stuffing.

## Output
At most one evidence-backed backlog proposal with affected pages and success criteria. Hand implementation to engineering. No publishing, domain changes, paid tools, link purchases or fabricated rankings. Missing Search Console access is a blocker to measurement, not a zero-traffic result.
