# Release standard

- The brief identifies a real audience and unmet need, with a bounded definition of done.
- Public claims, names and project links are supported by supplied or verified facts.
- Product source and public copy contain no private company context or credentials.
- The site works on mobile and desktop; keyboard navigation and visible focus work.
- ASCII art is decorative, can be paused and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Primary content and links work without animation or JavaScript.
- Relevant checks pass on the exact release revision. Record what was checked and limitations.
- The live URL serves the expected content; key links are verified after deployment.
- A previous known-good deployment is identified when one exists, with a rollback procedure.
- Record the release revision, URL and evidence in the private registry. Do not mark a draft as launched.
